What this is

AI Engineering from Scratch is Rohit Ghumare's open curriculum: 20 phases, 523 lessons, from installing a toolchain to shipping autonomous multi-agent systems. This site is an independent reading edition of it — the same words, the author's own interactive figures, rebuilt as a website you can actually finish.

What was added

  • A reader, not a repo. Lessons are pages with a live outline, reading times, prev/next paging and a progress bar.
  • Full-text search across every lesson, ranked heading-first (⌘K or /).
  • Progress tracking in your browser's local storage — nothing is sent anywhere, and Reset my progress in the footer clears it.
  • Build-time syntax highlighting, so a 523-lesson course still loads fast on a phone.
  • Brutalist design: hard edges, real contrast, one accent per module, and 3D only where it helps you recognise where you are.
